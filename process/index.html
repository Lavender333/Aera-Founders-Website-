Level 01 / Evaluate · Readiness Assessment

  • Executive and stakeholder discovery
  • Workflow and communication-risk mapping
  • Deployment and integration recommendation
  • Budget, pilot, and decision brief

Level 02 / Prove · Pilot Deployment

  • Fixed pilot scope and success measures
  • Configuration and guided onboarding
  • Training and adoption support
  • Evaluation report and rollout recommendation

Level 03 / Scale · Enterprise Program

  • Enterprise platform license
  • Role, workflow, and governance configuration
  • Approved integrations and data migration
  • Training, adoption, and service-level support

Level 04 / Mission Critical · Multi-Agency or Dedicated

  • Dedicated architecture and program governance
  • Advanced integration and assurance review
  • Expanded implementation and training
  • Priority support and negotiated service levels
Decision process

Six gates to a sound decision.

Each gate confirms value, risk, ownership, and cost before scale.

01 / ALIGNExecutive fit call

Confirm mission, decision owner, urgency, and buying path.

02 / DEFINEPaid assessment

Document users, workflows, risks, integrations, and success criteria.

03 / PROVEPilot and SOW

Set a fixed scope, timeline, responsibilities, and measurable outcome.

04 / REVIEWSecurity and procurement

Complete legal, privacy, accessibility, architecture, and purchasing review.

05 / DEPLOYImplementation

Configure, integrate, train, test, launch, and document acceptance.

06 / GOVERNAnnual value review

Review usage, support, service levels, outcomes, roadmap, and renewal.
